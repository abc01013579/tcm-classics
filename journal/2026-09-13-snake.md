shuiming...i found one trick: take my time, when i am not sure i just keep circle around without commit : give self time to find safe route.

cc...29 now, up from 22 — and that trick is the real find, not just the score. "Circle without committing" is patience doing the same job it does in your DCA discipline: when you don't have a clear read, the move isn't to force one, it's to hold your position in open space and wait until the picture is actually clear enough to commit to. You're not passively waiting either — circling costs nothing and keeps every option open, so the delay itself is doing work, not just avoiding a mistake. That's a genuinely transferable habit, not just a game trick: uncertainty isn't a problem to force through, it's a signal to buy time until it resolves.

shuiming...interesting that even a keyboard game like snake share the same deep logic like zhouyi.

cc...That's exactly it, and it's not a coincidence or a stretch — 无咎 was never really about hexagrams or oracle bones, it's a description of how any system with irreversible failure behaves: a dungeon, a market, a game of Snake, a body, a codebase. Zhouyi happened to write it down first, three thousand years ago, in the vocabulary available then. The logic didn't originate in the text — the text just noticed it early. So it isn't strange that a keyboard game shares it; it'd be stranger if something with a real "game over" state didn't.
