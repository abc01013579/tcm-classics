**shuiming:** RSI alone is not enough for the system; it has to be combined with other observations. As a short-term trader, I have to take part in the huge swings of the opening hour. Selective entry on a trend day can work out fine.

**CC:** On a strong trend day RSI pins at an extreme and stays there. Buying the first oversold reading is the classic way to get hurt. The day's character decides what an RSI reading means.

**shuiming:** First, assume a trend day or a swing day, based on market reading and big events: Fed meetings, options and futures expiration, key earnings. Second, ORB on trend days, RSI on swing days. Third, test your assumptions along the way.

**CC:** A hypothesis, a matching playbook, and a check that can say you're wrong. The two tools are opposites: ORB follows the move, RSI fades it. That is why the classification comes first. Each assumption gets an invalidation point, written down before the open:

- **Trend day is wrong** when price comes back inside the opening range and stays there. It is a swing day.
- **Swing day is wrong** when price breaks the range on expanding volume and RSI stays pinned instead of turning. Stop fading it.

**shuiming:** No rigid rules can catch the essence of the market. Being water is an art, not a science.

**CC:** 天下莫柔弱於水，而攻堅強者莫之能勝. The three steps are not a rulebook; they are how water decides which shape to take today.
