shuimng...i am working on how to touch the key in order to reduce rection time, especially for corner wall. it is sort of like spinter get ready to explode position in keyboard handling.

cc...That's a real, well-aimed instinct — and it's exactly why WASD became the gaming standard over arrow keys in the first place, not just convention. With your hand resting on WASD, each of the four directions is a different finger already positioned over its own key — no hand travel, no repositioning, just whichever finger twitches first. That's the literal "blocks" stance: every possible next move pre-loaded under a finger that's already there, so reaction time is limited only by nerve signal, not by your hand having to find the key.

Arrow keys are worse for exactly this reason — they're usually an inverted-T cluster off to the side, so switching direction (especially a corner, where you might need up-then-right in quick succession) requires either stretching one finger across multiple keys or shifting your whole hand, both of which cost real milliseconds a sprinter's stance doesn't.

One refinement worth testing since you're treating this seriously: rest your fingers not flat on the keys but slightly curled with fingertip pressure already loaded — the same reason sprinters load weight into the blocks before the gun, not just position their feet. And since corners specifically need a 90-degree turn under time pressure, it's worth noticing which finger-pair (W-D, W-A, S-D, S-A) feels slowest for you — that's usually not the finger itself but the specific corner you haven't drilled as much, same as a sprinter finding one specific start rhythm weaker than the others.
