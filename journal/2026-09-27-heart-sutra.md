cc.....That's a more precise structure than mine:

Body, three medical classics: 黄帝内经 (the theory), 神农本草经 (the herbs), 辅行诀 (the prescriptions linking herbs to the organs).

Root, 周易: the source that the others grew from. Medicine and the Yi share one origin (医易同源). Sun Simiao wrote: 「不知易，不足以言太医」 "Without knowing the Yi, one is not fit to speak of great medicine." The yin-yang and change behind the 内经 come from there.

Soul, 心经: the one that came from outside, from India through Xuanzang, and is therefore a real add-in to the native tradition. Its 色即是空 balances everything else. After caring for the body and understanding change, you let go of attachment to both.
Three for the body, one for the root, one for the soul. It's a complete person in five books.

shuiming.....heart sutra gives people the justification to quesrion even zhouyi and the bible. because they are all human delusion. test it in real universal setting.

cc.....Yes. What makes the Heart Sutra radical is that it doesn't exempt itself. It even negates Buddhism's own core teachings:

无苦集灭道，无智亦无得。
"No suffering, no origin, no cessation, no path; no wisdom, and no attainment."

The Four Noble Truths, the very foundation of Buddhism, are declared empty too. The Diamond Sutra says it outright: 法尚应舍，何况非法 — "even the Dharma must be let go of, let alone what is not Dharma." Teachings are a raft for crossing the river. Once you're across, you don't carry the raft on your back.

The Buddha also said "test it". In the Kalama Sutta he told people not to accept anything because of tradition, scripture or a teacher's authority, including his own, but to check it against their own experience. That's the stance you're describing.

One refinement matters, though. 空 doesn't quite mean "delusion" in the sense of "false". It means nothing has a fixed, independent, absolute nature. Everything exists in dependence on conditions. Buddhism keeps two levels of truth (二谛):

At the ultimate level, the Zhouyi, the Bible and the sutras themselves are empty: human constructions, not absolute truth. 

At the conventional level, some constructions work much better than others when tested. A raft is empty too, but a good one still gets you across.

That's exactly how you've lived. You tested the markets for 32 years until DCA proved itself. You test TCM on your own body. You kept the five books because they held up when you tried them, not because tradition told you to. Even in code, when you asked "how much faster?" in exercise 5-7, we didn't trust the claim. We measured it.

So the Heart Sutra isn't against the Zhouyi. It keeps the Zhouyi honest, so it stays something to use and test, not an idol to worship.
