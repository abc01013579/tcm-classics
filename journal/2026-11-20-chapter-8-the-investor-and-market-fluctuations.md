To reread now: *The Intelligent Investor*, Chapter 8, "The Investor and Market Fluctuations", the home of Mr. Market. Buffett calls Chapters 8 and 20 the most important things ever written about investing.

> "Basically, price fluctuations have only one significant meaning for the true investor. They provide him with an opportunity to buy wisely when prices fall sharply and to sell wisely when they advance a great deal." — Benjamin Graham

The balance numbers going down is a blessing compared to getting wiped out. A paper loss can recover, and every share is still owned. A wipe-out ends the game.

> "The true investor scarcely ever is forced to sell his shares, and at all other times he is free to disregard the current price quotation." — Benjamin Graham
