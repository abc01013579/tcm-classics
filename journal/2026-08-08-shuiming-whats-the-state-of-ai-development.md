Here’s where things stand as of August 2026:

Big shift: chatbots → agents
AI is moving from “answer my question” tools to systems that plan, act, check their own work, and come back when the job is done ￼ — basically colleagues you delegate to, not just tools you ask.

Models keep improving, fast
New releases are dropping constantly across all the major labs — GPT-5.6, Claude 4.6, Gemini 3.1, DeepSeek-V4, Llama 4 ￼ — and even smaller models are getting much stronger, with 7B models now outperforming last year’s 70B models. ￼

Getting cheaper
OpenAI cut GPT-5.6 pricing by 80%, ￼ and Google has pushed prices down too — AI is getting significantly more affordable to use.

Adoption is huge
Generative AI reached 53% population-level adoption within three years of ChatGPT’s debut — faster than the PC or the internet — with organizational adoption at 88%. ￼

Regulation is catching up
Europe just rolled out continent-wide rules requiring AI systems to identify themselves to humans, ￼ and the US is tightening rules too (deepfake laws, disclosure requirements for AI agents).

Bottom line: No AGI yet, but AI has gone from “smart chatbot” to genuinely capable agent that can do multi-step work — and it’s spreading into daily life faster than any previous tech.
