**shuiming:** News doesn't move the price. Money does.

**CC:** CL in April 2020 shows it plainly. The COVID news had been out for weeks; the price went to −$37 on one day because forced sellers had nowhere to put the oil and almost no one would buy. Price moves when someone has to trade. Tonight's NVDA earnings work the same way: the number is the news, positioning decides the move. Good news can sink a stock everyone owns; mediocre news can lift one everyone has sold.

**shuiming:** Actual money order flow is the essence.

**CC:** That's why the process starts with the open. The first hour shows the real flow: who is buying, who is selling, how urgently. A breakout that holds the opening range is money behind the move. Forecasts and opinions don't move price; executed orders do.
