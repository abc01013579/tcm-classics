Many coders have lost their jobs, and the worst is not here yet. How to survive this harsh reality? Find a lower-paying job, cut daily expenses, save as much as you can, etc.

I was replaced twice by technology revolutions. First, the internet revolution took away my stock brokerage job; second, the mobile revolution took away my yellow taxi medallion. I currently work at an Amazon warehouse as a T1 associate. I get paid $60,000 a year. It is below the New York average income, which is around $70,000 a year. But it pays my bills. Survive first, then grow.

Even masters are not safe. A well-known JavaScript author, with three books and 28 years of experience, wrote that he went from a six-figure income to living in his car after a startup bet and a lost job. One bad bet plus one layoff can take away everything.
