**shuiming:** What I learned from last week?

**CC:** The week started with SPY RSI trades and ended with everything folded into DCA. Six lessons, in your own entries:

1. **Survival first.** No margin means staying power. A 20% drawdown is ok; a wipe-out is not.
2. **Short-term trading hooks people.** $41,000 by luck, $24,000 lost by adding to a loser, all given back in 2025. The first live RSI trade was cut at −$52 in a free fall. RSI dropped; SPY is now part of DCA.
3. **Money moves price, not news.** NVDA beat earnings and still fell. CL went to −$37 because forced sellers had no buyers.
4. **Know the risk you own.** Buffett sold TSM over Taiwan, not the business. NVDA fell almost 50% in a week on its CEO question and an SEC probe, risks no shareholder controls.
5. **Temperament over IQ.** Graham's Mr. Market, Chapter 8. A free fall clears any delusional urges.
6. **You can't tell the kind of drop while you're in it.** 9/11 recovered in a month, 2020 in five months; 2000 and 2008 took years.

**shuiming:** Survival first, then growth. 无咎.
