I have been doing DCA for year and half. Last week it got tested in a free falling market and it stands firm and valid. For a long value investor, DCA is a much better way. I read Soros’ books. His way of investing is very hard to master. DCA is much easier intellectually, but it maybe as hard as Soros’ way psychologically.
